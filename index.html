Adaptive payment-gateway router

Razorpay · 2026

Problem
A gateway can start to fail at any time. Static rules continue to send the traffic to it until a person sees the problem, and this can take hours.
Solution
A UCB bandit with drift detection selects a gateway for each transaction and learns from each result. A safety floor stops all traffic to a gateway that fails.
Impact
In a simulation with 10 million transactions, the success rate was about 2.2 times the success rate of a threshold router. After a shadow test, the live traffic ran above the control.

Driver churn prediction

Namma Yatri · 2024–2025

Problem
Approximately 42% of the active drivers stopped to drive. The operations team found them only after one week with no activity, and many had already left.
Solution
A daily score for approximately 150,000 drivers, from an average of XGBoost and a random forest on 36 features. The operations team calls the drivers with the highest risk.
Impact
Against a randomised holdout, the drivers who got a call churned 30 percentage points less. Monthly churn for all drivers decreased from 42% to 30% in three months.

Driver safety enforcement

Namma Yatri · 2024–2025

Problem
Complaints about unsafe drivers arrive in three languages and are a small part of thousands of comments about fares and routes. Serious cases became visible some days late.
Solution
The Sarvam API translates the text, and a classifier gives one of five severity groups. A small problem decreases the priority of the driver, and a serious problem goes to a person who can block the driver.
Impact
Safety complaints decreased by approximately 50%, and the average rider rating moved from 4.6 to 4.8. The system gave a lower priority to approximately 12,000 drivers and blocked approximately 550 drivers.

Slack-native AI analyst

Razorpay · 2025–2026

Problem
A team of 127 persons asked the same data questions every day, and each question became a ticket for a small data team. The answers existed in more than 2,000 documents that nobody could find.
Solution
The documents became one approved data wiki. A model-agnostic Slack agent reads the index, opens the necessary pages, asks a question if the request is ambiguous, and runs read-only SQL.
Impact
96% of the answers got a positive rating. Approximately 40% of the team asked a question every day, and the median answer needed approximately 2 minutes.

Real-time payment fraud detection

Razorpay · 2025–2026

Problem
Suspect payments waited in a manual review queue. A person examined each one, and a decision took up to four days. The money moved during that time.
Solution
A model scores each transaction as it occurs. The clearest cases are blocked automatically and the payment is reversed. The other cases go to a person, who now has a much smaller queue.
Impact
The review turnaround decreased from approximately four days to hours.

Personal · 2026
