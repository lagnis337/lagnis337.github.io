Adaptive payment-gateway router

Razorpay · 2026

Problem
A gateway can start failing at any moment, and static rules keep feeding it traffic until someone notices, which can take hours.
Solution
A UCB bandit with drift detection picks a gateway per transaction and learns from every outcome, with a safety floor that cuts off any gateway that is clearly failing.
Impact
In a 10-million-transaction simulation it reached about 2.2 times the success rate of a threshold router. After a shadow test, live traffic ran above the control.

Driver churn prediction

Namma Yatri · 2024–2025

Problem
About 42% of active drivers were churning. Ops only spotted them after a week of inactivity, by which point many had already gone.
Solution
A daily score for roughly 150,000 drivers, averaging XGBoost and a random forest over 36 features. Ops call the highest-risk names on the list.
Impact
Against a randomised holdout, drivers who got a call churned 30 percentage points less. Monthly churn across all drivers fell from 42% to 30% in three months.

Driver safety enforcement

Namma Yatri · 2024–2025

Problem
Complaints about unsafe drivers arrive in three languages, buried in thousands of comments about fares and routes, and serious cases surfaced days late.
Solution
The Sarvam API translates the text and a classifier sorts it into five severity groups. Minor cases drop the driver's matching priority; serious ones go to a person who can block them.
Impact
Safety complaints fell by about half and the average rider rating moved from 4.6 to 4.8. Around 12,000 drivers were deprioritised and about 550 blocked.

Slack-native AI analyst

Razorpay · 2025–2026

Problem
A 127-person team asked the same data questions daily, and each one became a ticket for a small data team. The answers already existed across 2,000 documents nobody could search.
Solution
Those documents became a single approved wiki. A model-agnostic Slack agent reads the index, opens only what it needs, asks a clarifying question when the request is vague, and runs read-only SQL.
Impact
96% of answers earned a thumbs-up. About 40% of the team asked something every day, and the median answer took around two minutes.

Real-time payment fraud detection

Razorpay · 2025–2026

Problem
Suspect payments sat in a manual review queue. A person examined each one and a decision could take four days, while the money moved.
Solution
A model scores every transaction as it happens. The clearest cases are blocked and reversed automatically; the rest go to a person, now with a far shorter queue.
Impact
Review turnaround fell from about four days to hours.
