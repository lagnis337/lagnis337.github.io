Problem

Payment traffic is routed across several gateways whose reliability changes without warning. When a gateway degrades, static routing rules keep sending it traffic until someone notices — failed payments, lost revenue, and a detection time measured in hours. The task was routing that adapts on its own: detect degradation from observed outcomes and shift traffic, without needing to predict failures in advance.
