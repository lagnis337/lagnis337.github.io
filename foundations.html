Face-image compression with PCA

PCA via SVD on 400 Olivetti faces; reconstruction from K components. Mean squared error 0.0077 at K=10, 0.0022 at K=100 (~41:1 compression). Plus a sweep of K showing diminishing returns. 10/10

Linear regression: gradient descent vs. normal equation

Derived the mean-squared-error gradient and implemented batch gradient descent with feature standardisation; closed-form solution for comparison. Both predict $293,237 for a 1,650 sq ft house. Bonus: the descent trajectory on the cost surface and a learning-rate sweep showing convergence, oscillation, and divergence. 12/10
