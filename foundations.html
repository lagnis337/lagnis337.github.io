Linear Algebra for Machine Learning — UC San Diego, Division of Extended Studies

CSE X412.87 · 3 quarter units · Summer 2026 · Grade: A+ (4.0)

100% across all five quizzes and the final exam; both coding assignments at or above full marks. Syllabus, by week:

1–2Vectors, matrices, tensors; matrix operations, inverse, determinants
3Linear systems — column/row/null spaces, rank, Gaussian elimination, reduced row-echelon form, pseudoinverse, condition number
4Vector spaces — norms, orthogonality, projection, span, basis, linear transformations
5Gram–Schmidt orthogonalisation, QR and LU matrix decompositions, eigenvalues and eigendecomposition
6Singular value decomposition (SVD), low-rank approximation, the four fundamental subspaces
7Principal component analysis (PCA) — assignment: face-image compression
8Quadratic forms and gradient descent — assignment: linear regression, gradient descent vs. the closed-form normal equation
9Neural-network frameworks (PyTorch / TensorFlow demos)

Face-image compression with PCA

PCA via SVD on 400 Olivetti faces; reconstruction from K components. Mean squared error 0.0077 at K=10, 0.0022 at K=100 (~41:1 compression). Plus a sweep of K showing diminishing returns. 10/10

Linear regression: gradient descent vs. normal equation

Derived the mean-squared-error gradient and implemented batch gradient descent with feature standardisation; closed-form solution for comparison. Both predict $293,237 for a 1,650 sq ft house. Bonus: the descent trajectory on the cost surface and a learning-rate sweep showing convergence, oscillation, and divergence. 12/10
